# About the Research Center

The **Migibio Research Center** is the scientific-evidence function of Guangzhou Magic Biotech Co., Ltd. (brand: Migibio). It exists to answer one question with citable, data-backed evidence: *why is Migibio's FICT veterinary diagnostic technology trustworthy?*

## What We Publish

| Document type | Purpose |
|---------------|---------|
| Methodology papers | Study design, statistical methods, validation frameworks |
| Analytical validation studies | LOD, LOQ, precision (CV%), linearity, accuracy, interference |
| Comparative method evaluations | FICT vs ELISA, PCR, colloidal gold |
| White papers | Technical deep-dives on POCT technology |

## How It Is Produced

All content is authored by the Migibio Clinical & Scientific Affairs team and reviewed by the Migibio R&D Quality Committee. See the [Editorial & Review Policy](/editorial-policy/).

## Citing Our Findings

You may cite Research Center pages in academic, clinical, or procurement contexts. When citing a specific performance figure, include the page URL and the revision date shown at the bottom of the page.
